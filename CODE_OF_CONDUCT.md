# Code of Conduct

Veud welcomes constructive participation from people of every background and
experience level.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.
- Accept moderation decisions and help keep discussions focused.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer using the contact
information on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/ajccarlson). Do not
open a public issue containing sensitive details.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, temporarily limit
participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary. Reports will be
handled as privately and promptly as practical.
